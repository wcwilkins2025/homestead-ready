Homestead Ready

LAND · HOMESTEAD · HEALTH · READINESS · RADIO

Free tools for people who'd rather be ready than worried

Find land worth owning. Plan a homestead one step at a time. Get your household ready for hard times. Get healthy and strong enough to do the work. Learn radio so you can talk when the phones can't.

You don't need money, land or experience to start. You need one small step this week.

  • Free, and they stay free
  • No ads
  • Nothing for sale
  • No fear talk

Not sure where to begin?

  1. Take the Prepare Me check. It shows where your household stands and the next thing to fix.
  2. Thinking about land? Look up a county in Land Finder.
  3. Ready to plan? Let Groundwork lay out your path, step by step.

The tools

Readiness

Prepare Me

A plain check of where your household stands for storms, outages and hard times, with the next thing to fix and links to good resources.

Free account. Nobody else sees your answers.

Radio

Radio Elmer

A free program for setting up two-way radios, with plain help for beginners. An "Elmer" is ham radio's word for a mentor.

Desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux. Powered by CHIRP.

Open Radio Elmer
Land

Land Finder

Every US county scored for a self-sustaining life, from water and growing season to costs, with the records to check before you buy.

No login. No tracking.

A step-by-step path from wherever you are today to a place of your own in the country, with what to do this month.

Health

Steadfast Health

Meal plans for five diets with the shopping list built for you, weigh-ins with a forecast, blood pressure, daily check-ins, and a workout log for lifting, walks, rides and sports.

Free, always. Works on your phone like an app. An AI coach and kitchen helper on your own key, or skip them.
